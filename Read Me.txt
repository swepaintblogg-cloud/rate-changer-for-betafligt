FPV PROFILER – INSTALLATION

Extract the entire ZIP file, then run Install.cmd.
Open FPV Profiler from your desktop or Start menu.
Your saved profiles are stored in your Windows user account.

Click Read Drone to read current rates, or Edit Selected Profile to edit
library values. Choose Betaflight Rates or Actual Rates above the table.
Review and Write Edited Rates backs up and writes your edited settings,
then checks the values after restart.

Custom profile names keep the names you entered.
Windows system dialogs follow your Windows display language.
